# Reputation design (agreed with user, to build in v22)
Standing -100..+100 per faction (float). Start 0; Kre'tari -20.
Ranks: Hostile <=-50 | Unfriendly -49..-10 | Neutral -9..19 | Friendly 20..49 | Trusted 50..79 | Honored >=80
Gains: story mission +15 (chain finale +20); repeatable contract +6; killing the faction's attackers in its HQ +1.
Losses (user-reduced): damaging faction ships -2.5 (per provocation event); killing faction patrol/civilian -0.75 each;
  destroying the faction flagship -10; completing a mission against the faction -0.8.
Spillover: allies +50% of each change, enemies -50% (relations from factions.md).
Effects: Friendly +10% payout, flagship tolerance 25% (vs 10%); Trusted +20%, flagship defends player in HQ, secret weapon in HQ shop;
  Honored +35%, secret ship for sale. Unfriendly: HAIL offers only amends contracts. Hostile: flagship + patrols attack on sight, HAIL refused.
UI: Standings button (7 bars, rank title, perks); inbox message per rank change; HAIL greeting.
