# Space Corsair — Batch 1 notes

- This pack contains the seven faction flagship sprites, the Varn Syndicate Nebula Moth carrier, seven faction emblems, six weapon-family badges, and reputation.json.
- Ship canvases are 1024×1024 RGBA PNGs with genuine transparency, centered at 90% maximum silhouette fill. The flagships have eight empty round turret sockets each; the Moth has three empty sockets and two flush missile hatches. Engine nozzles sit at the stern edge.
- Emblems are 512×512 RGBA PNGs with transparency and a two-color faction palette, simplified for use at 48 px.
- Weapon badges are 256×256 RGB PNGs with the dark background, soft white glow, gunmetal hex and family accent color described in the brief.
- reputation.json uses the asset keys spartan, reaver, kretari, varn, ironclad, helion, and warden (warden is Frontier Authority). It contains six faction-flavored rank titles, the requested rank messages, eight repeatable contracts per faction, exactly two amends contracts offered at Unfriendly, and one secret weapon and secret ship per faction.
- Validation confirmed the JSON parses, all contract objective values use the allowed list, and every title, briefing, success message, rank message, weapon lore and ship lore meets its character limit.
