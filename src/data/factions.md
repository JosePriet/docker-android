# Faction Bible

## Spartan Republic

**Description:** The Spartan Republic began as a compact of inner-system settlements that pooled escorts after pirate raids stranded three worlds without food. Its founding charter still treats safe passage and civilian protection as public duties, but the elected fleet council now argues over how far security should extend beyond Republic lanes. Spartan doctrine favors disciplined formations, layered shields, and a visible command ship that can coordinate a mixed patrol. Its hulls are broad-shouldered, practical, and easy to repair, finished in steel gray, navy, and restrained gold. The SRS Aegis turns that language into a long central spine, paired armor flanks, and a clean white running-light strip. The Republic's allies are the Ironclad Militia and Frontier Authority; it remains neutral toward Helion and Varn, and hostile toward the Crimson Reavers and Kre'tari Brood. Spartan officers call this policy mutual defense, though their neighbors sometimes see an expanding perimeter.

**Flagship — SRS Aegis:** A steel-gray command titan with shielded flanks and a white spine light, built to anchor a Republic defense line.

**Commander — Ilya Venn:** A patient strategist who measures every victory by the civilians who make it home.

## Crimson Reavers

**Description:** The Crimson Reavers formed when debt-bound convoy crews mutinied against owners who treated ships and workers as replaceable cargo. They have no central charter; captains follow a rough code of shared spoils, crew loyalty, and retaliation for betrayal. A Reaver captain earns authority by keeping a crew fed and a ship flying, not by inheriting a title. Their vessels are rebuilt from captured hulls, with mismatched armor, exposed hardpoints, blunt bows, and heat-scarred red plates over black metal. Furnace-orange lamps and patched plating make each ship recognizable without making the fleet uniform. Bloodforge is the largest expression of that scavenged, defiant style. Reavers have no formal allies. They stay neutral toward the Varn Syndicate when a contract pays, and hostile toward the Spartan Republic, Kre'tari Brood, Ironclad Militia, Helion Combine, and Frontier Authority. A truce lasts only as long as both sides can explain its price to their crews.

**Flagship — Bloodforge:** A rust-red titan armored in scavenged black plate, its furnace lights burning like a warning across the hull.

**Commander — Captain Mara Vey:** A blunt, fiercely loyal captain who trusts a paid promise only after it survives a hard day.

## Kre'tari Brood

**Description:** The Kre'tari Brood is an ancient migratory species organized around living nests rather than borders or individual governments. Its ships carry brood chambers between safe worlds, and its memory keepers preserve history through shared signals and inherited instinct. The Brood regards survival of the young as its first law; expansion is usually a search for habitable nesting ground, though outsiders often mistake it for an invasion. Kre'tari craft are grown in linked, rounded segments instead of assembled from human-style plates. Their silhouettes resemble a central armored body with tendrils, bead-like escorts, and violet bioluminescent nodes. The Brood-Mother's pulsing purple hull is both command vessel and mobile nest. The Kre'tari have no recognized allies or neutral partners and regard every other faction—the Spartan Republic, Crimson Reavers, Varn Syndicate, Ironclad Militia, Helion Combine, and Frontier Authority—as hostile. Individual ceasefires can occur, but the Brood does not mistake temporary silence for trust.

**Flagship — Brood-Mother:** A violet, many-segmented living titan that carries a nest at its core and answers threats with the whole Brood.

**Commander — Nhal-of-the-Third-Egg:** A deliberate Brood-Voice who treats every living signal as a memory worth protecting.

## Varn Syndicate

**Description:** The Varn Syndicate began as a mutual-insurance compact among merchants who needed reliable routes between isolated systems. Over time its claims offices acquired escorts, then stations, then enough private force to decide which contracts were enforceable. Varn ideology treats predictability as the highest public good: every risk should be priced, every obligation recorded, and every route made profitable. Its ships are symmetrical, carefully serviced, and built around protected cargo and command cores. Dark violet armor, pale reactor lights, and narrow gold details signal wealth without the Reaver's visible disorder. Syndicate Crown is a mobile headquarters shaped like a sealed ledger. Varn is allied with the Helion Combine, neutral toward the Spartan Republic, Ironclad Militia, and Crimson Reavers, and hostile toward the Kre'tari Brood and Frontier Authority. The Syndicate prefers influence through debt and access, but will use force when it believes a rival has made its contracts meaningless.

**Flagship — Syndicate Crown:** A violet command titan with a bright central reactor, polished gold trim, and the composed silhouette of a moving headquarters.

**Commander — Director Sera Voss:** A cool negotiator who can make a threat sound like a routine clause in a contract.

## Ironclad Militia

**Description:** The Ironclad Militia grew from mining settlements that could not wait for distant governments to protect their rigs and families. Its crews are workers first and soldiers by necessity, with authority shared among elected settlement councils and veteran patrol leaders. The Militia values repair, mutual aid, and holding ground long enough for neighbors to evacuate. Its ships favor thick armor, visible reinforcement ribs, straightforward weapons, and parts that can be replaced in a belt-side workshop. Weathered olive, iron gray, and work-light amber dominate the fleet; little is polished and everything has a practical purpose. IMS Bastion is less a sleek warship than a mobile wall for the settlements it serves. The Militia's allies are the Spartan Republic and Frontier Authority; it is neutral toward Helion and Varn, and hostile toward the Crimson Reavers and Kre'tari Brood. Ironclad accepts outside help, but resists any partner who mistakes a rescue agreement for ownership.

**Flagship — IMS Bastion:** A broad, sand-gray fortress titan reinforced with field-built armor and bright work lights along its shielded hull.

**Commander — Marshal Dax Rourke:** A steady veteran who speaks plainly and never asks a crew to risk what he will not.

## Helion Combine

**Description:** The Helion Combine grew from independent shipyards and courier firms that pooled research, standards, and convoy protection to reach markets beyond the inner systems. It remains a federation of companies, but shared infrastructure gives it more reach than its members could claim alone. Helion's ideology is pragmatic: keep routes moving, make technology dependable, and let a successful contract fund the next one. Its ships are tapered, light, and efficient, with clean paneling, bright running lights, and engines designed for speed over heavy armor. White and silver hulls carry controlled orange or blue accents that make each service fleet legible at a glance. Sunspire is an elegant long-spined flagship that turns the Combine's solar engineering into a public emblem. Helion is allied with Varn, neutral toward the Spartan Republic, Ironclad Militia, and Frontier Authority, and hostile toward the Crimson Reavers and Kre'tari Brood. The Combine prefers open trade, but it will fight when a threat closes a route on which entire settlements depend.

**Flagship — Sunspire:** A slender white titan with a brilliant central light and swept fins, designed to keep Helion's trade routes open.

**Commander — Flight Director Lio Serrin:** An upbeat route planner who weighs danger carefully but hates wasting a clear runway.

## Frontier Authority

**Description:** The Frontier Authority began as a loose network of independent rangers who marked safe passages and answered distress calls beyond settled space. It became a formal service only after repeated disputes over who had the right to close a route or abandon a stranded crew. The Authority's charter puts rescue, clear navigation, and equal access ahead of territorial claims. Its patrols are small, disciplined, and expected to operate far from support. Warden ships use long, clean hulls, reinforced noses, spare external fittings, and cool blue-white panels that remain visible against dark space. FAV Warden embodies that approach: a simple, forward-focused command ship built to be found when someone needs help. The Authority's allies are the Spartan Republic and Ironclad Militia; it is neutral toward Helion, and hostile toward the Crimson Reavers, Varn Syndicate, and Kre'tari Brood. It cooperates with larger powers when routes stay public, then pushes back when security becomes a pretext for tolls or annexation.

**Flagship — FAV Warden:** A pale blue-white patrol titan with a narrow forward profile, built to mark and protect the frontier routes.

**Commander — Ranger-Captain Anja Vale:** A calm field leader who would rather bring everyone home than win an argument about jurisdiction.
