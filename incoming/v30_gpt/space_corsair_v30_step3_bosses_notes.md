# Space Corsair v30 — step 3 bosses and titans

`bosses_v30.json` was completed before the art. It contains 3 bosses and 7 faction titans, with English and Latin American Spanish names, one-line mechanics descriptions and exactly two callout lines per enrage. The text uses only the fixed mechanics in TASKS_v30.md.

All sigils, enrage overlays and titan signatures are grayscale-on-black for code tinting. Each `titan_signature_*` has a quiet centre for compositing behind the corresponding hull. `titan_decal_*` uses the matching faction emblem as transparent RGBA; the Kre'tari and Varn emblems came from the earlier supplied v22 art set because this v30 ZIP contains only five of the seven emblems.

The two markers and rarity beam are grayscale-on-black. `icon_boss.png`, `icon_elite.png` and `ui_boss_plate.png` are RGBA. Rotate sigils around canvas centre; do not interpret the illustrations as new mechanics.
