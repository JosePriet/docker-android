Space Corsair mod v45 (build 52, sc-mod-workspace 113d24f). Install over v44 or a v45 test build; your save is kept. Both players need this same APK.

- Multiplayer is back in the flight menu (top-left button, Multiplayer): local games on the same Wi-Fi, and internet games. Host a private game and send your friend the 4-character code, or host a public game. Direct connection when the networks allow it, otherwise through the relay.
- Pirate havens (red-map sectors): no police patrols or waves; police no longer turn on you for fighting pirates or for stray hits.
- Leveling and XP: stock-pace XP curve (existing pilots are re-leveled once), mission levels on the 0-40 scale, sector enemies inside their level band, ship unlock levels and new ship prices.
- In-game updates (flight menu, Updates): later content and game updates download inside the game, no full reinstall.
- Plus everything from the v45 test builds: internet play, whole-game audit fixes, QoL set, sector events and kill chain, co-op rules.
