# Space Corsair mod v44

- LAN co-op: on the docked station toolbar, tap "LAN co-op". One phone hosts, the other joins (same Wi-Fi or one phone's hotspot). Both phones need v44. The guest flies in the host's sector with its own ship, save and shields, and shares the host's XP.
- PvP duel: the host taps "PvP duel". Rounds end at 1 hull; nobody dies.
- Guest radar shows the host's enemies, loot, rocks and mission targets; enemy life and shield bars show on the guest.
- Missions: same mission = done together (the host completing it completes it for the guest; claim it at a station before quitting). Different missions: the guest taps "Fly my own missions" in the LAN co-op dialog; "Follow the host" rejoins.
- Phoenix Lance (Legendary, Spartan HQ): unlocks when a Monolith reaches form V; elites, bosses and police Monoliths use it.

# Space Corsair mod v43a

## New: a playable prologue

A fresh save no longer starts in the tutorial. It starts in a fight you cannot win.

You are a level 25 Corsair flying the Corvette battlecruiser, and you have taken a 2,000,000 credit bounty on the
**Bloodforge**, the Crimson Reaver flagship, in the derelict player bay — a sector that is not on the galaxy map and
cannot be reached from anywhere in the game. You have two minutes before Reaver reinforcements arrive. Its armour
holds against anything you carry.

When the clock runs out, or your hull gives, the Bloodforge finishes you, Genesis salvage recovers your pod, and the
game begins properly: licence revoked, savings seized, one loaner hull, the tutorial sector.

Nothing the prologue does touches your save. It plays once, on a fresh save only.
