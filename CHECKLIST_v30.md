## Claude (code)
**Map**
- [x] Wire `incoming/map_territory/*` (territory fields + border) into `work/modart` as lossless WebP

**Shields**
- [x] Faint shell at rest; segments dim as charge drops and flicker or sputter below 25%; fizzle-out at 0 (no shatter)
- [x] Recharge pulse around the rim; segments relight with charge; one full pulse lap during the 1.3 s reboot; hit ripple at the impact point
- [x] Regen curve: full item rate at ≤25% charge, ~75% at 50%, ~55% at 75%, ~40% near full; the half-rate state halves the whole curve
- [x] Enemies: same curve at half rate; no regen after reaching 0 unless boss or special skill

**Wrecks**
- [x] Cruiser-size hulls and up split into 2–3 pieces cut from their own sprite, with burnt edges, debris, venting and sparks
- [x] Pieces are physical: collide with NPCs, enemies and asteroids, and can be shot apart
- [x] Loot only when the player destroys a wreck
- [x] Player death: 3–4 s break-up before respawn; the player's wreck is visual only and is removed
- [x] Enemy wrecks persist and are lootable

**Loot**
- [x] Base table by hull type and enemy level and type; small hulls drop modestly even at high level
- [x] Elite (star before the name): good or high-ranked gear whatever the hull size
- [x] Bosses: one guaranteed good item from their pool, plus a chance of rare or secret
- [x] Per-sector, per-owner modifier that only changes the odds of high-rarity drops

**Derelicts**
- [x] Place the sites from `derelicts.json` (fixed wrecks with lore, scan text and entry line), lootable under the same rules

**Bosses**

- [x] Each boss's unique enrage triggers once at low hull
