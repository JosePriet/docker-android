# ASSUMPTIONS

This is a deterministic damage-exposure model, not an Android physics emulator. Formula ports and table conformance are testable; absolute gameplay timing is **not validated** by the supplied emulator summary. See `validation.md`. No damage, accuracy or resistance multiplier was fitted to those observations.

## Inputs, authority and versions

- Repository Java and `codex/data/` are authoritative. Their bytes match the uploaded source/data snapshot. Attached prose is research input, not additional instructions. The original `EnemyCalc.java.txt` is evidence and is never executed by the simulator.
- All 47 entries in `hulls.tsv` are included. It contains literal backslash-t separators, not actual tab bytes; the loader supports both. JSON contains actual built gear requirements, including mod items already rescaled to levels 7/22/37; do not rescale again. Existing stock item requirements and secret weapons' literal level 15 remain as exported.
- `enemy-calc-out.txt` wins over rounded prose or a newer source change. Its printed numbers are the oracle, including random-tier averages, subclass elite factors, pilot multiplier, flagship 3× damage, police 1.2× shot and rate, and drone averages. Printed `k` values have only thousand-unit resolution. No precision is invented behind them.
- Ordinary enemy numbers are loaded, not independently regenerated. The JSON has gear and XpBuilder, not the stock enemy constructor tables. Conformance validation therefore checks the loader; capital hull/overshield recomputations are separate independent checks. Source revisions such as `ModQol.policeLevel`'s later patrol scaling do not override the explicitly requested older oracle.
- Enemy truth exists only at L10/20/30/40. Other enemy levels fail with a clear message rather than receiving invented/interpolated stats. Player fitting works at every level 0–40.
- There is no separate alien-titan stat row. `alien titan` resolves explicitly to the Kre'tari flagship Brood-Mother. It receives the flagship doomsday budget, not a fictitious alien-titan profile. `titan` resolves to Police titan SRS Aegis. All exact names are listed by `enemies`.
- `ModCaps` in this snapshot is a visual/profile class, not a combat damage-cap implementation. Actual doomsday caps are in `ModTitan.doomHit`; LAN declaration ceilings in `ModLanCheck` police network packets and are not PvE damage reductions.

## Stock/closed-game calls and missing exported state

The repository supplies stock smali, allowing read-only confirmation of several calls that were opaque in the uploaded Java. These are not assumptions: `PlayerShip.applySoloGear` sets hull from `playerMaxLife * lifeBooster`, copies gear laser damage/reload, and sets cannon damage to the item's integer damage; `PlayerShip.shoot` fires a fixed pair; `PlayerCanon.requestShoot` emits two projectiles per turret per full reload when `tirGroupe=true`, as set by `ModShips.base`; one beam tick uses its own path. `ModFitting.fittedDps` omits this factor of two in its UI estimate, so live stock firing takes precedence. `Shield.applyDamage` consumes shield before hull and resets its no-regeneration timer. The archive includes the relevant read-only stock smali excerpts as provenance.

Remaining calls/state whose numerical behavior is not fully specified by JSON/Java:

1. **PlayerInfo build state** (`PlayerShip.applySoloGear`, `PlayerInfo.playerMaxLife`): full hull starts from the supplied XpBuilder level-life row. No character upgrades, storyline stat bonuses, timed buffs, consumables, critical hits or alteration reload multiplier. Baseline pilot ATK/DEF/REA are `ModV34.statBase(level)`. Level shield multiplier applies to regeneration only. This reproduces the measured 446,656 Leviathan and 143,568 Warlord hulls.
2. **Nested gear effects** (`LaserSpec.alterationSpecs`, `ShieldSpec.bonuses`, stock `Projectile.resolveAlteration`, `Shield.applyDamage`): exported JSON omits these nested objects. Their proc chance/damage, shield full-charge damage bonus, laser reload reduction, reflection, damage resistance, fast regen and repair bonuses are not assigned invented defaults; these optional effects are excluded from the build model. `standardLaserLevel1WithCrit`, for example, uses only its exported direct stats. This can affect the ordering of 'best' gear. The numerical direct-stat results are not a claim to reproduce those omitted effects.
3. **Missile collision** (`Missile.onImpactWithElement`, stock splash): direct damage plus `radiusDamage` both land on the primary target. No splash on other ships, anti-missile interception, homing misses, recoil, projectile lifetime, distance or flight delay. Siege missile direct and radius damage both receive `ModTitan.onMissile`'s 1.9×. Ammo is unlimited, auto-fired on reload; inventory caps are not ammunition throughput limits. This strongly favors the fast percuteur launchers. Emulator comparisons disable missile firing because the summary specifies auto-firing turrets but does not identify a missile build or missile usage.
4. **Native enemy shield construction** (`EnnemisSpec.shieldSpec`, `FrelonShip.shield`, `Shield.doPhysic`): the oracle gives total shield but not native shield item/dead time/rate. Use the last exported decent shield's recharge/capacity ratio (50/1500 per second), zero unexported dead time, and `ModShield.rate`'s half-rate charge curve; native shields stay down once empty. These parameters are explicit approximations, not recovered native Frelon stats. Big `ModV34` shields use the same ratio and the exported capital regen whenever present; they regenerate from zero at 85% under player fire for 3 seconds.
5. **Ordinary enemy firing** (`EnnemisCanon`, `TaupeShip`, `FrelonShip`, `EnergyLaser`): oracle DPS already integrates burst cadence, subclass elite reloads and stock raw-spec turret/beam behavior. The ordinary table has no per-shot damage/reload rows. Integrate its average rate each 0.05 s; no synthetic enemy reload is invented. Player guns/missiles and capital batteries use explicit source/gear reloads. Non-capital alpha strike and regen opportunities between real bursts cannot be recovered from average DPS alone.
6. **World/target selection** (`ObjectRegistry`, collision, turret auto-aim, nearest enemy, spatial skill checks): one stationary enemy within range; every direct shot hits and every firing mount bears on it. Spread and travel are ignored; precision is not a probability. Fixed guns fire a pair; every mod turret fires a grouped pair per reload (`PlayerCanon.requestShoot` with `tirGroupe=true`). Triple layouts indicate mount placement, not three sequential shots. The UI `ModFitting.fittedDps` undercounts live turret projectile DPS by two. Initial weapon salvo is available at time zero. Enemy continuous rates begin after the first timestep.
7. **Unexported class spec arrays** (`EnnemisSpecByLevel`, `CivilSpecByLevel`, `ShieldSpecBuilder.decentShieldSpecByLevel`): the oracle overrides source re-derivation. No attempt to infer hidden class-table values from an unrelated player gear row. Native/big shield-rate inference is the explicit exception described in item 4.
8. **Death/respawn pipeline** (`Element.applyDamage`, `applyProjectileHit`, stock kill callbacks): hull <=0 ends the actual duel; no invulnerability, resurrection, respawn, kill loot or mission state. Percentage doomsday finishing damage is applied directly instead of invoking the Android kill-credit path. This preserves the requested damage budget, not the bookkeeping side effects.
9. **Floating point / clocks**: player gear modifiers emulate Java float32 multiplication and its staged integer truncations at turret/reload assignments. Combat integration uses Python double precision, with due events snapped to the next 0.05 s grid. Periodic deadlines accumulate their actual period rather than adding a rounded grid period. This avoids a systematic fire-rate bias. Exact Android frame order/roundoff is not reproduced; numerical timings have at least one timestep uncertainty.

## Explicit modeling choices for visible mod mechanics

These are scope/geometry choices, not unavailable closed-code formulas:

- **Refits:** use `ModV35.scores/classAverage/picks/mults`, excluding the developer hull from averages. Only two strongest scores are picked; cargo participates and repair never does. Ties retain earlier stats. Faction traits stack multiplicatively. Faction counterfactual override changes weapon fit and outgoing/incoming counter identity only; innate refit picks/trait remain those of the original hull.
- **Stage:** defaults to `ceil(refit/2)`; optional stage override changes hardpoints independently. `ModV38` plans are honored (Leviathan 4/5/6, Monolith 2/3, Bastion 1/2, Hornet 2/3). No additional stage DPS multiplier is invented. Other stage passives (Hornet Stinger/wing shields, Monolith aura and specialized multipart skills) are excluded; this limits predictions for those hulls.
- **Fitting:** `ModV34.weaponRule` applies 0.6 per-hit damage and 0.6 fire rate for off-faction families. Favorable innate damage/reload bonuses are removed in that case, but shield and missile bonuses/refit remain. Neutral hulls receive only 0.85 per-hit. Spartan rails gain 1.1 damage and Frontier rails get 0.9 reload time. These are distinct from faction counters, resistances and normal-enemy `ModV35.ttkFactor`.
- **Best gear:** highest steady single-target direct-stat DPS at/below pilot level and within `ModV34` laser slot size; disintegrators scored at their full ramp; shield maximizes capacity, then item recharge; missile maximizes `(direct+radius)/reload`. This is a defined comparison policy, not a search for minimum duel TTK or maximum survival with limited ammo. Prices, standings, hull licences and secret unlock quests are unconstrained. Developer/debug gear excluded, including `devDebugLaser`. Hulls above the pilot's purchase level, retired industrial hulls and the DEV hull are included because the request asks for every hull; label these as comparison builds, not obtainable progression builds.
- **Locked gear / specialized weapons:** `ModLock.requiredShip` excludes alien beam/bolt and Megatron gear from this mod-hull table. Regent Hive Bay needs a separate two-drone, finite-lifetime, stance/targeting model; it is not silently treated as a zero-DPS missile. Phoenix Lance has a separate heat/burn system and is also explicitly rejected/excluded. Thus 'best' is scoped to the modeled direct weapons, not every specialized loadout in the mod. Explicit ordinary beams can be fitted numerically, using one gun, but their stock locking/travel/alteration effects are outside the model.
- **Capital batteries:** source `ModCapitals.GUNS` supplies family, reload and salvo weight; rounded oracle DPS normalizes battery damage once, without reapplying 3×. Police titan cadence is 1.2× rate and is already reflected in the table's overall DPS. Equal battery types/mounts fire synchronously; a three-spine salvo is simultaneous (no intraburst delay is specified in `ModCapitals.fire`). Warden damage splits in proportion to each source gun's DPS. Average Aegis drones stay alive and their table DPS is integrated continuously; the player never switches targets to kill them. No extra 3× is applied to either batteries or drone rows.
- **Capital defenses:** Aegis shots arrive within its 50-degree bow wall, so `ModTitanAI.damageIn` applies 0.1; `--rear` disables it. Ironclad has four 3%-hull armour plates, 70% soak including overkill of the breaking plate, restored once below 30% hull. Varn's 4-second cloak begins at its initial 7-second timer and repeats every 15 seconds. Other spatial offensive flagship skills (ram, lance, mines, artillery strikes, supernova, snipe, mirror swarm), movement and drone targeting are excluded. Their source formulas are visible but positions/contacts are not supplied; no arbitrary skill hit rate is chosen.
- **Enemy signatures:** direct fleet flak uses all five 25% shrapnel hits (worst-case contact), Kre'tari acid uses its full two-second 10%/s effect as steady extra damage, Helion switches 1.5 shield / 0.7 hull based on shield at impact, Warden pauses regen two seconds. Acid timing is averaged and cannot reproduce short-fight ticks. Pirate/unnamed stock rows have no known faction by `ModV34.factionOf`, so no faction is invented. Flagships are excluded from these fleet signatures by `ModEnemyGuns.shooter`; Varn's battery still uses `ModWeapons` disintegrator ramp. Police waves lack a sector-faction parameter in the exported table and remain faction-neutral; no sector gun signature is guessed.
- **Police:** 25% armour for ordinary/patrol police, 30% for titans, both before overshield. Overshield is exported 40% max hull, resets quiet timer on direct hull damage too, and refills after 20 seconds quiet in six seconds. Titan shield-break multiplies current player shield by 0.4 after first 9+1.2 seconds, then every 18 seconds. Cruiser/BC drain removes an extra half incoming damage from the player shield and stops regeneration three seconds. Escalation helpers port current `nextTop` (+1 class per wave), wave composition and countdown rules; legacy `unlockedClass` heat thresholds are exposed separately and do not gate current escalation; the duel does not spawn reinforcements. Lockdown/snare/pin/jump jam have no effect in a stationary one-target duel. Police membership overrides wheel and family resistance in both directions, not the player's own weapon-fit multiplier.
- **Anchor wall:** stationary stage-IV/V Leviathan plants after 2.5 seconds; all enemy shots cross its front arc. Capacity is 1.5× fitted shield, incoming damage 0.65 (0.5 during doomsday charge/fire); a blocked breaking projectile is fully destroyed. The empty wall refills over 25 seconds then replants. Plant/fold animation collision timing and partial angular coverage are approximated; `--no-anchor` disables it. Movement/dodging experiments cannot be reproduced by this geometry.
- **Doomsday:** default policy presses the manual button as soon as available; `--no-doomsday` models never pressing it and is used for emulator checks. Initial readiness is 20 seconds (DEV 3). Leviathan's executable `ModLevDoom` sequence overrides stale skill text: 5.4 s charge, 4.6 s fire, 0.15 s damage delay, 2.2 s vent, 90 s subsequent cooldown. Weapons stop while charging/firing/venting. Each percent tick spends at most 1/22 of the per-shot budget. Leviathan budgets: titan 10%, flagship/boss/capital 15%; others 7%/10%. A shorter non-Leviathan activation can use fewer than 22 ticks and therefore less than the ceiling. Ordinary targets take mitigated 100,000 damage/s instead. Percent slices bypass both shields, armour and resistances. Crimson Throne receives `ModTitan.kind`'s titan ability despite its old `h.doomsday=false` TSV flag. Range/aim are assumed valid throughout the beam.
- **Reporting clocks:** TTK and TTD are uninterrupted independent-exposure clocks: after the first death the other side's damage continues counterfactually, solely to provide both columns. `Duel` and end-hull snapshot stop at the first death. Simultaneous deaths are mutual; shots at t=0 can give a zero TTK. `>180` means right-censored at 180 s, not infinite invulnerability. Re-run with `--max-time` for longer fights. 'Survive' does not include recovery after winning.

## Emulator discrepancy interpretation

Run 112/113 are different controlled builds/behaviors; individual police-titan point times and Warlord target/time pairs are absent. Validation prints exact Leviathan flagship observations and group interval comparisons only. It does not invent per-target timings from ranges. Warlord's harness forcibly mounts an XL railgun which normal slot rules reject; the validator explicitly bypasses slot-size validation only for that recorded build.

All-hit source-derived rail-only offense predicts substantially longer flagship kills than the summary reports. Missing missile/other source attribution or a build/harness discrepancy is a hypothesis, not an established explanation. Geometry generally reduces applied damage and cannot by itself explain faster measured kills. Incoming deaths are usually earlier under uninterrupted all-hit damage than in moving-target emulator runs. Without raw logs/attack attribution, no exact cause or hidden multiplier can responsibly be asserted. The 25% comparison failure remains visible; use the simulator for conditional formula comparisons, not a claimed calibrated emulator forecast.
